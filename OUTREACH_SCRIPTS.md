# Outreach Scripts (Messenger / WhatsApp / Phone / Dekha kora)

Sob script e `[ ]` er bhitore jinis ta nijer moto bodle nao. Bhasha ta nijer moto sohoj rakho, robot er moto na.

---

## Script 1: Messenger / WhatsApp, prothom message (website nai emon clinic)

> Assalamu alaikum, ami Sirajush, Mymensingh e thaki, website developer.
> Apnader [clinic er naam] er Facebook page dekhlam, bhalo kaaj korchen. Kintu website nai, tai rogira Google e khujle apnader khuje pay na.
> Ami clinic er jonno ekta sample website baniyechi, [link]. Mobile e dekhle bujhben, doctor list, test er daam, ar online appointment shob ache.
> Apnader jonno o ei rokom baniye dite pari. Ekbar dekhben?

**Keno kaaj kore:** problem diye shuru, sample link ache, choto proshno diye shesh.

---

## Script 2: Follow-up (2-3 din por, reply na ashle)

> Assalamu alaikum [naam/sir/madam], ager message ta dekhechen ki?
> Sample website ta dekhle 2 minute lagbe, pochondo na hole kono shomossha nai.

Ekbar-i follow-up koro, 5 din por aro ekbar. Er beshi na.

---

## Script 3: Phone call (30-40 second)

> Assalamu alaikum, ami Sirajush, Mymensingh theke bolchi. Ami website banai.
> Apnader clinic er Facebook page dekhlam, kintu website nai dekhlam.
> Ami ekta sample baniyechi, apnader moto clinic er jonno. WhatsApp e link pathiye dei? 2 minute dekhle bujhben.
> Pochondo hole kotha bolbo, na hole kono shomossha nai.

**Tip:** call er por-i WhatsApp e link pathao, jate bhule na jay.

---

## Script 4: Shorashori dekha kora (sobcheye kaaj kore)

Phone/laptop e sample ta open rekhe jao. Receptionist ke bolo:

> Assalamu alaikum, ami Sirajush, Mymensingh er website developer. Owner/manager sir er shathe ektu kotha bolte pari? Ekta choto kaaj niye, 2 minute lagbe.

Owner ke:

> Sir, ami apnader clinic er jonno ekta sample website baniyechi, ektu dekhen. Rogira Google e khujle apnader khuje pabe, doctor er shomoy ar test er daam dekhte parbe, ar online appointment nite parbe.
> Apnader doctor/test er info diye dile 7 din e live kore dibo.

**Sathe nao:** phone e sample, ekta business card/naam-number likha chit.

---

## Script 5: Reply paoar por, "Daam koto?"

> Amar package: 5-page website (home, doctor, test price, appointment, contact), mobile friendly, WhatsApp/call button, Google Map, Bangla.
> Daam [10,000] taka, 7 din e delivery. Shuru korte 50% advance, baki ta live hoyar por.
> Shuru korle apnar doctor list, chhobi, ar test er daam pathiye den.

Daam jiggesh korle **kom daam e shuru koro na**, package ar value bolo. Prothom 1-2 ta client e discount dite paro, kintu bodole review/testimonial chao.

---

## Script 6: "Ekhon dorkar nai / pore bolbo"

> Thik ache sir, kono shomossha nai. Ami sample link ta pathiye rakhi, jokhon dorkar hobe bolben.
> Ar jodi kono doctor ba onno business er kotha jaanen jader website dorkar, amake ektu refer korle khushi hobo.

Refer chaowa onek client ashe.

---

## Script 7: Doctor ke directly (nijer chamber ache)

> Assalamu alaikum Doctor sir, ami Sirajush, Mymensingh er website developer.
> Apnar chamber er jonno ekta choto website baniye dite pari: apnar naam, degree, chamber er shomoy, location, ar appointment er jonno call/WhatsApp button.
> Rogira Google e apnar naam khujle sohoje khuje pabe. [link] e sample dekhte paren.

## Kichu niyom

- Rogira ba clinic er private data (rogir nam, report) kokhono nijer kache rekho na ba share koro na.
- Sample e kono asol clinic er naam/logo/chhobi use korbe na.
- Kono daam/service er mithya promise dio na (jemon "Google e number 1 hoye jabe").
- "Free" website dibo bole shuru koro na, sample dekhao, kaaj shuru hobe advance er por.
